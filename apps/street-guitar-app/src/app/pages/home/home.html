The Creator

David Dunavant & the Street Guitar Method

David Dunavant has spent 27 years teaching guitar with a single focus: getting students to play real music, fast. As the author and creator of the Street Guitar curriculum, David built a method that cuts through the noise — practical, authentic, and designed for people who actually want to play.

  • 🎓 Music degree, Univ. of Arkansas + Master's, Bowling Green State University
  • 📖 Author & creator of the Street Guitar curriculum
  • 🏆 Founder of Guitar Wars — bringing live music to communities across Arkansas
  • 📰 Featured in Arkansas Money & Politics
  • 🔧 Professional guitar repair and setup services available
