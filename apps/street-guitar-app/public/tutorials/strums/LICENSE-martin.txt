The guitar sound in every app: shared/sounds/38-85.mp3 and every strum in shared/strums/,
except woodblock.mp3, mute-down.mp3 and mute-up.mp3 (those are David's)

Source: github.com/sfzinstruments/Discord-SFZ-GM-Bank, Discord GM/Melodic/026-Acoustic Guitar (steel).sfz
(2017 Martin HD28 Vintage Series single notes, recorded by Jeff Learman).
License stated in that file: Creative Commons CC0 (public domain dedication). No credit required.
The open chord strums were built by Cowork on Oct 25 2026 from those notes; every other strum and every
note between the 15 samples was made from them by Claude Code (the sfz's nearest sample, retuned).
Every file is raised to true pitch (A440) by the samples' measured cents (they were recorded 4 to 15 cents flat). Same CC0 status.
